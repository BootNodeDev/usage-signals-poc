Setup

    Sign in with a Stellar wallet

    You sign a short text challenge that names the service, a nonce and an expiry. No password is collected.

    Signed-in account
    Sample account

    Notification channel

    One channel, used for service notices only: breach, sub-processor and handover notices, token expiry and a failed domain re-check.

    Prove control of the domain

    Any one of the three methods is enough. Events are accepted only from pages on a domain you have proved. Proofs are re-checked on a schedule.
